Peru

Peru is a country in western South America, bordered by Ecuador and Colombia to the north, Brazil to the east, Bolivia to the southeast, Chile to the south, and the Pacific Ocean to the west. It is home to a rich mix of cultures, from ancient Incan history at Machu Picchu to vibrant modern cities like Lima, the capital. Peru's diverse geography spans coastal deserts, the Andes mountains, and the Amazon rainforest.
